Adding the new `-race` workflow surfaced three previously latent failures:
**not** caused by v1.7 work, but worth tracking:

- `backend/session/TestRunPostLoginExpectAutomationDrainsStaleOutputBeforeNextStep` — data race on the test's own channels
- `backend/session/TestContainerExecSessionPipes` and other TestOutputLogger* — race between test goroutines and the session's read/emit goroutines
- `backend/store/TestSkillsStore_List_ConcurrentReads` — intermittent "unexpected end of JSON input" likely from shared tempdir races across test cases

These three tests pass under `go test -count=1 -run <test>` in isolation. They should be tracked under v1.7 follow-up.
